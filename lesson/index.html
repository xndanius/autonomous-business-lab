The lesson · 13 October 2026

Build a business that runs while you sleep.

You used the 60-Second Startup in class: one idea became a live page. Here is how that can become a business.

1Task vs loop 2One loop up close 3Three loops 4You stay in control 5Become AI native 6Your turn 7Next

1 · Task vs loop

A task ends, but a loop runs again.

Asking AI to write one page is a task: one job. A loop repeats the same steps for each new request. The 60-Second Startup did this for each student's idea.

2 · One loop up close

Your idea became a live page in four steps.

A second AI checked the work before it went live. A failed check should stop delivery and ask you.

3 · Three loops

Three loops help a business grow, serve and improve.

Serve is the loop you just saw. To turn the startup into a business, add two more loops.

Grow
Each page invites someone else to make their own.
Serve
Each new idea becomes a checked, live page.
Improve
Ask users to rate their pages, then test one change to make the next page better.

4 · You stay in control

You choose the goal and can stop the loop.

For the startup, set a spending limit and rules for what can go live. A second AI can miss mistakes, so test it. Keep a stop button.

5 · Become AI native

AI native means working with AI every day.

Describe what you want and where you are stuck. Ask for one small step, then try it. Check the result yourself before moving on.

“My startup page has no price. Help me add one, explain the change, and show me how to test it.”

Send ideas in class and design a loop at home.

In class, send as many ideas as you want to the live idea board. The best appear on the screen, scored 0–2 on each of five checks. At home, use this prompt in any AI chat, then write your six lines.
