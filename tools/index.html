Before you publish

Check that you may use the result commercially. Never imitate a real person or a real brand. Say that AI made it.

For simple images, start with the image tools inside ChatGPT or Gemini.

5 · Keep up

AI news on X, in 15 minutes a day

New tools and models ship every week, and most of them appear on X first. Set it up once.

  1. Make an X account for AI news only. Keep your personal feed separate.
  2. Follow the starter list. Fifteen accounts are enough to begin.
  3. Put them in an X List called “AI”. Read the List, not the “For you” feed.
  4. Turn on notifications for the two or three tools you use every day.
  5. Don't get a post? Ask Grok. Reply “@grok explain this simply” or “@grok is this true? Show sources.” The answer is public, so ask nothing private.
  6. 15 minutes a day. Bookmark what you want to try. Try one new thing every week.

Use them safely

Never paste a password, an API key or someone else's personal data into a chat or a terminal. Keep keys in the tool's secret settings. Tell your users when AI made the result.
